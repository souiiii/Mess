## Thali photograph

- File: `client/public/images/thali.png`
- Created using the built-in image-generation tool for this project.
- Used on the sign-in screen and student overview, with a serving-suggestion label.
- This is an illustrative generated image, not a photograph of the institute's kitchen or its current menu.

Prompt direction: Photoreal editorial food photography, overhead Odia vegetarian thali on a dark charcoal tabletop with off-white linen. Stainless-steel plate with rice, dalma, aloo, curd, cucumber and chapati. Natural directional light, restrained natural colours, detailed food texture, clean magazine composition. No text, people or logos.
